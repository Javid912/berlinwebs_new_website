Create a premium interactive hero illustration for a modern Digital Growth & Business Intelligence agency.

The visualization should represent a complete business growth ecosystem rather than a simple network diagram. It should combine the concepts of a Star Schema, Data Warehouse architecture, neural networks, business intelligence pipelines, and digital marketing into one elegant animated system.

STYLE
- Minimalistic and premium.
- Clean vector graphics.
- Futuristic but professional.
- Soft glowing effects.
- Smooth flowing animations.
- Inspired by Vercel, Stripe, Linear, Figma, and modern SaaS landing pages.
- No clutter.
- Plenty of whitespace.
- Use the existing website design system, colors, typography, spacing and visual language.
